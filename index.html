DetailMargin Privacy Policy

Effective: October 14, 2026

DetailMargin is an Android app for preparing vehicle detailing estimates. This policy describes how the current offline version handles information.

What you put in the app

You may enter business contact details, customer names and contact information, vehicle descriptions, quotes, direct costs, private condition notes, and vehicle photos. These are saved locally on your phone. The app does not operate cloud synchronization or require a user account.

How information is used

Information is used on your device to calculate customer quote prices, save records and photos, and generate customer-facing estimates. The app does not use ads or analytics SDKs and does not automatically upload customer records to the developer. No customer data is sold by the app developer.

Sharing is your choice

When you choose Share, Android lets you send quote text, selected photos, or a branded PDF through another app. Those receiving apps have their own privacy practices. Before sharing, review the estimate and images for personal details including names, license plates, faces, and addresses. The current customer-facing PDF does not include direct cost amounts, contribution margin or private condition notes.

Photos and permissions

The app uses Android's camera and document-picker interfaces to let you capture or select photos. It saves its own resized photo copies in app-private storage, without deleting the originals. The Android app does not request general camera, contacts, device location, microphone, or Internet permission.

Retention and deletion

Quotes and stored photos remain on your phone until you delete a quote, clear the app data, or uninstall. The app has no cloud backup service and Android automatic backup is disabled in the app. For now, keep copies of essential business records outside the app.

Children

The app is intended for business users and is not directed to children.
